Platform Answers

DERSec, in plain terms.

Concise answers for teams evaluating DER interoperability, validation, and OT cybersecurity.

What is the DERSec platform?

DERSec is an end-to-end platform for distributed energy resource interoperability and OT cybersecurity. It connects DER assets, models expected behavior, validates interoperability and performance, and secures operational fleets using shared device and protocol context.

How does DERSec cover the DER lifecycle?

DERSync connects and normalizes DERs, DERSim models device and fleet behavior, LabTest Pro validates communications and performance, and Sentry applies that context to OT cybersecurity.

Which DER environments is the platform designed for?

DERSec is designed for connected energy environments including solar inverters, battery energy storage systems (BESS), EV charging infrastructure, microgrids, AI data center energy systems, DERMS, VPPs, and other grid-edge assets.

Which standards and interfaces fit the DERSec ecosystem?

DERSec is designed for DER ecosystems using standards and interfaces such as IEEE 2030.5, DNP3, OCPP, REST APIs, and certification workflows including IEEE 1547.1 and UL 1741 SB. Exact protocol and standards support depends on the DERSec product and deployment.
